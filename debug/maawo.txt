Ideally located for commuting or outdoor activities, MAA Western Oaks is a terrific place to live in Austin. Surrounded by nature, residents enjoy being outdoors at the pools, picnic, and dog park, where all breeds are welcome. Apartment homes come standard with smart home technology, vaulted ceilings, modern kitchens, and an in-unit washer and dryer. Catch the latest indie film just steps away at Alamo Drafthouse or unwind over casual bites and drinks at neighborhood favorites like Backspin Texas Sports Bar & Grill, 400 Rabbits, and District Kitchen + Cocktails. For shopping, explore big-name retailers at Sunset Valley Village or browse more fashion-forward options at Barton Creek Square. Nature lovers will appreciate the miles of trails and green spaces at Zilker Park and Dick Nichols Park, or a colorful stroll through the wildflower meadows at the Lady Bird Johnson Wildflower Center. For a dose of art and culture, enjoy live performances at the Mary Moody Northen Theatre or discover outdoor sculptures and peaceful garden paths at the Umlauf Sculpture Garden + Museum. With quick access to MoPac, you’re only minutes from downtown, while still coming home to the laid-back, friendly feel of Western Oaks. Plus, the community is zoned for three excellent schools in the Austin school system, making it a perfect place for families.

Community Perks

Two gorgeous swimming pools provide an ideal spot for relaxing on sunny days. Our cabana, grilling area, picnic area, and outdoor firepit are perfect gathering spots to enjoy quality time with neighbors and friends. For even more ways to unwind or connect, the clubhouse offers a welcoming atmosphere for casual hangouts or special get-togethers. When you need to catch up on work or focus on a big project, take advantage of the business center and conference room, or grab a pick-me-up from the cozy coffee bar. Our 24-hour fitness center provides state-of-the-art fitness equipment for those who like to stay active on their schedule. Plus, MAA Western Oaks is nestled near a serene nature preserve, offering residents a tranquil escape right at their doorstep. All dog breeds are welcome; the dog park is large enough for your pooch to romp around in our pet-friendly community. Attached garages and detached garages are available among the countless amenities at our luxury apartments for rent in MAA Western. A Courtesy Officer and 24-hour on-call maintenance team are always nearby to help with whatever you need. With parking options including attached and detached garages, you'll always have a convenient place to come home to. Practical comforts, like easy package receiving, smooth trash and recycling services, and attentive pest control, help you spend more time enjoying life and less time on chores.

Apartment Features

The stylish and luxurious one-bedroom, two-bedroom and three-bedroom apartments boast open floor plans, walk-in closets, and an in-unit washer and dryer. You will also find features like smart home technology, gas fireplaces, gourmet kitchens, wood floors, balconies, vaulted ceilings, and more. Your kitchen will feel like the heart of your home, with your choice of warm oak or crisp white cabinets, granite or laminate countertops, and a striking backsplash that ties everything together. Stainless steel appliances, a built-in microwave, and thoughtful touches like ample prep space make cooking and gathering both effortless and enjoyable. Bathrooms include a classic tub-shower combo. Large double balconies or inviting sunrooms in select homes let you enjoy the outdoors without leaving your space, and a fenced backyard option offers even more privacy and room to relax. Built-in bookshelves and desks provide stylish ways to stay organized, while cordless blinds and a programmable thermostat help you keep your home comfortable and bright. Ceiling fans and the warmth of a gas fireplace complete the picture, adding comfort and charm throughout every season.
