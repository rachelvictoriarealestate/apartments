Experience Living at The Oscar

Finding the right apartment should feel exciting, and at The Oscar, you'll discover studio and 1-bedroom floor plans designed to fit the way you live. Located near East Riverside, our thoughtfully designed apartment homes combine modern style with everyday comfort, all in a location that keeps you connected to the best of Austin. Whether you're looking for a cozy studio or a spacious one-bedroom apartment, you'll find open, inviting layouts with contemporary finishes that make it easy to settle in and feel at home. As a pet-friendly community, The Oscar welcomes your furry companion, so everyone can enjoy your new space together.




If you're searching for East Riverside apartments, you'll love the convenience of living just minutes from downtown Austin, Lady Bird Lake, and local restaurants, entertainment, and outdoor recreation. Take a look through our available floor plans and find the home that feels right for you. Whether you're starting fresh, simplifying your lifestyle, or just looking for a place that puts you close to everything, The Oscar offers a comfortable home in one of Austin's most

connected neighborhoods.




All floor plan pricing reflects base rent only. Additional monthly fees and one-time charges may apply and vary by apartment home, lease term, and selected options. Please review the fee details below or contact our leasing team for a personalized quote.
