Luxury Apartment Floor Plans in Downtown Austin

Find your perfect space at The Quincy, where luxury meets versatility in the heart of downtown Austin. Choose from studio, one-, two-, and three-bedroom floor plans, each designed with refined finishes and sweeping views of the city, lake, or historic Rainey Street. Penthouses offer even more with soaring ceilings and private balconies that invite light and skyline vistas into your daily routine.

Whether you're working remotely from your tech bar or hosting friends in your open-concept kitchen, every detail here supports a lifestyle of comfort and convenience. Enjoy walk-in closets with built-in shelving, under-counter beverage centers, frameless glass showers, and the flexibility to live and work moments from Lady Bird Lake Trail and the Convention Center.

Explore our floor plans below and reach out with questions about rates or availability.
