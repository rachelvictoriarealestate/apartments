MAA South Lamar is the center of all that's great about living in Austin. Residents flock to three pools, a poolside kitchen, and a dog park welcoming all breeds. The luxury apartment homes have smart home technology, modern kitchens, EV charging stations, and more! Step outside and explore one of Austin’s most iconic green spaces at nearby Zilker Park, or head to Lady Bird Lake just a mile away for kayaking and scenic trails. In South Lamar, you’ll find local favorites at every turn—savor sushi at Uchi, catch live country dancing at Broken Spoke, or unwind over pasta at It’s Italian Cucina. Downtown and Barton Creek Mall are both close when you’re in the mood for shopping, dining, or catching a show. During festival season, you’ll love the easy access to ACL and SXSW. Meanwhile, cultural favorites like the Umlauf Sculpture Garden + Museum, Warren Wildlife Gallery, and The Long Center for the Performing Arts are all just a short drive away. And when it’s game day, the excitement at Texas Longhorns games in the Moody Center is never far from home. The neighborhood is also zoned for the excellent Austin Independent School District, making it great for families.

Community Perks

Enjoy the sunset on the rooftop deck with views of Downtown Austin. Swim in three resort-style pools or entertain guests at our poolside alfresco-style kitchen. Gather around the outdoor fire pits on cool evenings or relax in the peaceful courtyard for a quiet moment away from the buzz of the city. Work out in one of the two 24-hour fitness centers or head outside to the walking trail. Or stay connected with AT&T Fiber, Google Fiber, and complimentary WIFI hotspots. The clubhouse offers a welcoming space to unwind or meet up with friends, while the coffee bar and business center make working from home both easy and enjoyable. Daily life feels seamless thanks to conveniences like an interior mail room, secure package lockers, package receiving services, valet trash pickup, and proactive pest control. Our 24-hour on-call maintenance team is ready when you need them, and planned resident events add fun ways to meet neighbors and feel at home. The community even boasts on-site restaurants and EV charging stations. Plus, pups of all breeds and sizes love the urban dog park in our pet-friendly community! With remote access, 24/7 customer care, self-monitored security, and smart notifications, you can get real-time information about your new home with smart home technology. Bike racks make storing your ride simple, and both garage and guest parking ensure everyone has a spot when they arrive.

Apartment Features

Our spacious studio, one-bedroom, and two-bedroom homes offer modern fixtures, including a smart home system, faux wood flooring, and in-unit washer and dryer. The kitchen is designed to make life easier and more enjoyable, with espresso cabinets, granite countertops, and stainless-steel appliances that bring a polished finish to the space. A backsplash, built-in microwave, side-by-side refrigerator, and ice maker help make meal prep and hosting feel seamless, while options like a fixed or moveable kitchen island offer flexibility for cooking or gathering. Bathrooms include your choice of a garden tub, glass shower, traditional shower, or tub-shower combo. Walk-in closets and built-in desks keep things organized and functional, while high ceilings and large windows with cordless or wood blinds fill each home with natural light. Finishing touches like barn doors or sliding doors, a programmable thermostat, and lighted ceiling fans help create a comfortable, inviting space that feels uniquely yours. Some units also have concrete floors, built-in bookshelves, and extra-large balconies. Call us today to learn more about our unit availability and offerings through Austin's S.M.A.R.T. Housing Program!
