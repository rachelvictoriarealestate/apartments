Building overview
Experience a new take on life at Urban East. This expanded community of studio, one, two and three bedroom apartments in Austin offers a brand-new phase with new residences and amenities, complete with sleek and sophisticated interiors, resort-worthy community amenities and an active social scene that's close to all your favorite city hot spots. Spacious and airy floor plans highlight a sleek interior feel, complete with high-end finishes like granite countertops, luxury vinyl flooring, and stainless-steel appliances. Whether you're relaxing by the swimming pool, socializing at the onsite bowling alley, taking in the view from the skyline lounge or heading out to enjoy neighborhood favorites near Lady Bird Lake, you'll find something for everyone at Urban East.
