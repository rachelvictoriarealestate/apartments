East Austin Energy

A bowling match in the game room, a lap in the pool, or an hour on the rooftop watching downtown come to life. Urban East elevates every moment between work and down time. Choose from studio, 1-, 2-, and 3-bedroom apartments, including brand-new homes with upgraded finishes. Each residence features granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and windows that pull the skyline into your living room. Outside, the courtyard grills are ready for weekend BBQs, the dog park doubles as a meeting spot, and East Austin’s restaurants and music venues are close enough to check out on a whim.
