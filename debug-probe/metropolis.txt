AFFORDABLE AUSTIN APARTMENTS WITH CREATIVE VIBES – WELCOME TO THE METROPOLIS

Looking for an affordable apartment in Austin, TX that captures the city’s vibrant energy? The Legendary Metropolis Apartments offers a truly unique living experience just 2 ½ miles from Downtown Austin. Our community embraces art, music, individuality, and the true "Keep Austin Weird" spirit all while providing easy access to public transportation, grocery stores like HEB Plus, local eateries, and Austin’s best attractions.

COLORFUL, CREATIVE, AND COMMUNITY-FOCUSED LIVING

The Metropolis isn’t your typical Austin apartment community. It’s a creative, welcoming space for individuals from all walks of life, whether you’re an artist, musician, student, professional, or simply looking for an affordable place to call home in Southeast Austin.
Here, self-expression and community go hand in hand. Residents can enjoy live music, connect through community events, and take advantage of our outdoor stage for performances. We also support in-home rehearsals from Noon to 10 PM, making it easy to create and collaborate.
At The Metropolis, everyone is invited to be part of a vibrant, inclusive environment where creativity thrives, and neighbors feel at home.

PRIME LOCATION NEAR EAST RIVERSIDE AND SOUTH CONGRESS

Our apartment complex is conveniently located in the East Riverside corridor, just minutes from South Congress, Lady Bird Lake, and downtown hotspots. Whether you're commuting to the University of Texas, Austin Community College, or just heading out for a night at Emo’s, The Metropolis puts you in the heart of everything.
