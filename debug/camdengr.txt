Life at Camden Gaines Ranch

Camden Gaines Ranch offers one and two bedroom apartment homes in Austin, TX nestled in the Barton Creek Greenbelt. Newly renovated apartment homes feature white quartz countertops, white shaker cabinets, grey subway-tile backsplash, stainless steel appliances and modern hardware as well as wood-style flooring throughout. Select apartments feature sweeping greenbelt views, fireplaces, built-in shelves, and walk-in showers. Floor plans that include extensive balconies and flex spaces allow you to create an at-home oasis or set up the perfect work from home spot. If you need a little extra space, we offer private garages with storage.

After a long day, come home and relax by the pool and outdoor lounge areas. If you are looking for a more active lifestyle, we have a 24-hour fitness center as well as direct access to walking trails and nature preserves. You and your pet can enjoy the spacious, landscaped community or surrounding lush greenspace. Plus we have electric car charging stations available for residents.

In addition to top-notch amenities, residents also receive a technology package included in the total monthly rent with a savings of up to 58% off retail.
