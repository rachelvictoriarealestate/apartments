Studio, 1, 2, & 3-Bedroom Floor Plans in Austin

Welcome to The Bowie, where luxury meets urban living in the heart of Downtown Austin. We offer a diverse selection of studio, 1, 2, and 3-bedroom apartments and penthouse suites, all with stunning views of the city.

All our residences are elegantly designed for an elevated living experience in Austin. Each home is built with stone countertops, stainless steel appliances, in-unit washer/dryers, and built-in wine racks and pantries. Find select floorplans with built-in desks and cabinetry and private balconies that overlook the city. Our penthouse suites have even more luxuries, such as elevated ceilings, floor-to-ceiling windows, and newly upgraded appliances. Along with beautiful high-rise views, our home amenities emulate a luxurious Austin lifestyle.

No matter your preference, The Bowie offers a range of floorplans to fit your needs. Find yours below to get started and schedule a tour to let us introduce you to your next home in Downtown Austin.
