Building overview
Discover a new way of living at Crosstown, located in the heart of the vibrant East Riverside district of Austin, TX. Our community represents the epitome of modern, comfortable living, perfectly blended with the excitement and convenience of East Austin's lifestyle. Crosstown's prime location puts you in the midst of the city's best, from leisurely strolls on the boardwalk at Lady Bird Lake to vibrant evenings on Rainey Street or exploring the historic charms of East Austin. Each of our apartments is a masterpiece of design, boasting stunningly crafted interiors and lifestyle-enhancing amenities that cater to your every need. Whether you're looking for a cozy studio, a spacious one-bedroom, or a luxurious two-bedroom apartment, Crosstown offers a variety of options to suit your personal style and preferences. As a pet-friendly community, we welcome your four-legged friends to join in the comfort and excitement. Living here means easy access to the best of Austin, including proximity to the airport, making travel a breeze. Embrace the energy of East Riverside, the serenity of nearby parks, and the convenience of urban living. Crosstown is not just an apartment community; it's a gateway to experiencing the best of Austin's east side living. Join us and take your lifestyle in an exciting new direction at Crosstown.
