The Oscar

Welcome to The Oscar, your next apartment community nestled in the heart of vibrant Austin, TX! Whether you're looking for a cozy studio or a spacious one-bedroom apartment, The Oscar has the perfect floor plan to meet your lifestyle needs. Our community boasts stunning views and a range of luxurious amenities designed to enhance your living experience. At The Oscar, we prioritize your comfort and convenience. Enjoy peace of mind in our smoke-free environment with 24-hour availability and online services for easy management of your apartment. You’ll appreciate the convenience of electric car charging stations and hassle-free public transportation access right at your doorstep. Our apartments feature stylish finishes, including granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and walk-in closets, ensuring that you have everything you need at your fingertips. Stay cool in the Texas heat with air conditioning and a ceiling fan, while your spacious eat-in kitchen makes entertaining a breeze. Enjoy our community's recycling program and feel good about living sustainably. Additional features such as cable readiness, washer/dryer hookups, and a secure key fob entry system make The Oscar an ideal place to call home. Don’t miss out on the opportunity to experience upscale living in one of Austin's most sought-after locations. Find your new home today—browse our availability or schedule a tour to visit our community! Your dream apartment awaits at The Oscar!
