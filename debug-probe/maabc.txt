Residents of MAA Barton Creek luxury apartments for rent in the Barton Hills neighborhood, southwest of Austin, enjoy incredible community amenities, including a gorgeous pool, grill area, fitness center, and dog park where all breeds are welcome. All apartments have smart home technology, an in-unit washer and dryer, and so much more. Just steps from home, you can explore the lush trails of Barton Creek Greenbelt or head to Zilker Park to enjoy festivals, live music, or a relaxing afternoon outdoors. Nearby restaurants make it easy to grab a great meal—Chinatown Westlake offers classic favorites, Rudy’s “Country Store” and Bar-B-Q serves up local barbecue staples, and Lou’s Barton Springs is a neighborhood favorite for casual drinks and bites. For shopping, Barton Creek Square provides everything from big-name stores to quick dining spots, while South Congress Avenue offers a lively mix of boutiques, vintage shops, and local cafes. Art and culture lovers will appreciate easy access to the Umlauf Sculpture Garden + Museum, Austin Nature & Science Center, Blanton Museum of Art, and The Long Center for the Performing Arts. And when it’s time for game day excitement, you’re just a short trip from Texas Longhorns games at the Darrell K Royal – Texas Memorial Stadium. Plus, the community is in the excellent Eanes school district, making it a great choice for families. Welcome home!

Community Perks

The resort-style pool has an arbor and fountain for a soothing atmosphere. Stay connected while you lounge or work poolside with community WIFI throughout shared spaces. Take in the beautiful Barton Creek Greenbelt views, then stop by the clubhouse for a relaxing break or to meet up with neighbors. The dog park, where all breeds are welcome, includes a dog wash station to groom your furry friends in our pet-friendly community. The grilling and picnic areas are all shady and inviting. The 24-hour fitness center is well-equipped to help you stay fit. Plus, you can’t beat the Barton Creek Greenbelt views. Daily life is easier with thoughtful services like package receiving, convenient recycling options, and routine pest control to keep your home comfortable. You’ll also have support from a Courtesy Officer and on-site maintenance team, ensuring peace of mind any time you need it. Join planned resident events to connect with your community and enjoy the flexibility of attached garages that make coming and going a breeze.

Apartment Features

MAA Barton Creek offers spacious one, two and three bedroom townhomes for rent with smart home technology and stylishly updated finishes. Choose from one-, two-bedroom, and three-bedroom apartment homes with an in-unit washer and dryer and attached garages. Your kitchen is ready to impress, featuring rich espresso cabinets, granite countertops, and a backsplash that adds just the right amount of personality. Black appliances, a built-in microwave, and an ice maker make cooking and entertaining feel effortless, while a roomy pantry keeps everything organized and within reach. Bathrooms are designed for both function and indulgence, with double vanities, options for a relaxing garden tub, a spa-like glass shower, or a classic tub-shower combo. An open floor plan, high ceilings with crown molding, and screened patios or balconies in every home—with some even offering a double balcony. Gas fireplaces bring cozy warmth on cool evenings, and thoughtful details like walk-in closets, linen closets, cordless blinds, lighted ceiling fans, and digital thermostats ensure every corner of your home feels comfortable and refined.
