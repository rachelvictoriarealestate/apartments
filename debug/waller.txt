Building overview
The Waller Symphony Square is setting a new standard for high-rise living in downtown Austin. The Waller combines sophisticated design, luxurious materials, innovative technology, and an unmatched location to deliver a living experience that is equal parts glamour, comfort, and convenience. Discover your new studio, 1, 2 and 3 bedroom apartments. Built to incorporate and preserve Austin's historical Symphony Square, The Waller is an iconic new downtown landmark at the heart of the City's burgeoning Innovation District. Direct access to the new Waterloo Greenway, Waterloo Park, and Moody Amphitheater create unmatched opportunities for recreation, entertainment, and community connection. Contact us today to find your next perfect home!
