At Windsor on the Lake, we offer a variety of sophisticated floor plans designed to suit your unique lifestyle. Choose from meticulously crafted one and two-bedroom apartment homes that range from cozy urban retreats to expansive residences perfect for entertaining. Each home features elegant finishes, soaring ceilings, and oversized windows that frame breathtaking city and lake views. The open layouts create a seamless flow, while custom cabinetry, granite countertops, and high-end appliances add a touch of refinement.

Select residences boast additional luxuries such as private verandas, walk-in showers, and floor-to-ceiling glass windows that maximize natural light. Every detail is designed with your comfort in mind, from the wood plank flooring that enhances the modern aesthetic to the spacious closets that provide ample storage. Whether you’re seeking a sleek city escape or a sun-drenched lakeside haven, Windsor on the Lake offers the perfect space to call home.

With an ideal location just steps from the Rainey Street District, residents enjoy easy access to Austin’s best dining, shopping, and cultural hotspots. The nearby boardwalk and trails provide endless outdoor recreation, while major employers and tech hubs are just minutes away. Contact us today to find your dream home at Windsor on the Lake.
