Gables Republic Square brings together luxury apartment living and boutique hotel energy in the heart of Downtown Austin’s Warehouse District. Through its unique partnership with Hotel ZaZa Austin, residents enjoy a lifestyle that feels elevated, social, and distinctly Austin. Located directly across from Republic Square Park and near Lady Bird Lake, Whole Foods Market, live music venues, rooftop bars, and some of the city’s best restaurants, the community places you in the center of the action while still offering the convenience of a walkable neighborhood. Whether you are strolling the Republic Square Farmers Market with your furry sidekick, soaking in the excitement of SXSW, or enjoying a stress-free commute through Downtown Austin, Gables Republic Square makes everyday life feel connected to the city’s energy.

Inside the community, studio, one-, and two-bedroom apartments feature sleek modern finishes, open-concept layouts, large windows, and thoughtfully designed spaces that feel stylish yet comfortable. Residents can unwind at the rooftop pool overlooking downtown, recharge in the fitness center, or take advantage of the lively atmosphere created by neighboring Hotel ZaZa. Gables Republic Square offers a luxury apartment experience that blends Austin’s creative spirit, walkable downtown lifestyle, and upscale hospitality into one unforgettable place to call home.
