Studio, 1, 2 & 3 Bedroom Apartments in Austin

From studios to penthouses and everything in between, 700 River offers a collection of residences designed with care and sophistication. Each of our studio, 1, 2 & 3 bedroom apartments in Austin is crafted to high standards, featuring thoughtful details and refined finishes for a truly exceptional living experience. Have a question or want to learn more? Our team is here to help.



Introducing Total Monthly Leasing Price

Planning your budget is essential, and we’re here to make it simpler. Our Total Monthly Leasing Price reflects all fixed, mandatory fees alongside your base rent, giving you a complete picture of your monthly costs. To customize your Total Monthly Leasing Price and plan with confidence, use our Calculate My Costs tool found within the Map view. We’ve also provided a list of all potential fees you may encounter as a resident which can be found at the bottom of the page.

Transparency meets convenience—so you can focus on finding the perfect home.
